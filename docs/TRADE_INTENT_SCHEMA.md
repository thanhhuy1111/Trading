# TradeIntent Schema Specification

## 1. Overview
`TradeIntent` is an analytical trade proposal generated by the Meta Allocator.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - `TradeIntent` is an analytical proposal ONLY.
> - Status is strictly fixed to `PENDING_RISK_REVIEW`.
> - Schema strictly excludes `quantity`, `notional`, `leverage`, `margin_mode`, `order_type`, and `client_order_id`.
> - Cannot be submitted to exchange APIs or executed without Milestone 6 Risk Governor approval and Milestone 7 Execution Engine.
